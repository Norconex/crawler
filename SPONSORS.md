# Sponsors

Norconex Crawler is free and Apache-2.0 licensed, and it stays that way.
Sponsorship funds the maintenance that keeps it that way — and puts your name
in front of the people using it.

👉 **[Become a sponsor](https://github.com/sponsors/Norconex)** ·
[What sponsorship includes](https://crawler.norconex.com/support#back-the-project)

Sponsorship is recognition, not a support contract. It carries no response
times and no committed engineering time. If your team needs those, see
[Support](https://crawler.norconex.com/support).

## Backing the project without spending anything

Most of the ways to help are free, and they matter:

- ⭐ **Star this repository** — the clearest public signal that a project is
  worth maintaining, and how a good number of people find it in the first
  place.
- 💬 **Answer someone's question** in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/Norconex/crawler/discussions) — free support
  works because people answer each other.
- 🛠️ **Contribute** — a bug fix, sharper docs, or writing about what you built.
  See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
